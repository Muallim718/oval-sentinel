# Revision 60: turn sooner and finish a weak attacker

This version combines the two observed attention cues from revision 59
with the short finishing window from revision 56 on the 4.50 controller.
Sound and real damage bearings help find a pursuer. Once an observed
attacker is already lined up and within a few loaded rounds of defeat,
the weapons keep tracking it through normal gun cooldown.

The short finish yields to protected repair, a held laser charge, or a
new attack outside the verified firing lanes. It selects the existing
combat target and trigger logic. Steering still gets its normal route
from the early equipment and recovery controller.

The separate feedback cases exercise both mechanisms. Earlier acquired
contacts can reduce rear damage, and a ready finish can remove one firing
lane before the other enemy gets another volley. The practice service
measures placement across the full match.
