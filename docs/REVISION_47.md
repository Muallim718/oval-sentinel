# Revision 47: arm while the first gun fight is still open

This version applies the short equipment route to the 4.31 recovery
controller. It can reach a visible secondary before the first magazine runs
out, while still aiming and firing the gun. The move requires a legal short
corridor, enough health for the equip delay, and a single credible gunner
far enough away. A quiet repair window has priority.

In the four continuous public-map cases, it takes the same available
lightning at 0.80 seconds and kills the first hunter at 5.40 seconds, with
770 to 860 HP. The 4.31 reference loses both starts with the harder-to-reach
pickup, and collects the other pickup later. The new pilot defeats the
second hunter in three cases and clears all three waves in one, finishing
with 171.78 HP after incoming rounds resolve. The exact late outcome varies
with navigation timing. These are limited tests of acquisition and sustained
combat, and the official service scores placement.
