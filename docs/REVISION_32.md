# Revision 32 permits a close instant arc during active return fire

While recovering, the original policy limits return fire beyond 28 m to
the gun. In the interval between 28 m and the end of lightning range,
this can leave the instant arc idle while an opponent keeps interrupting
healing. The new exception admits the arc only during existing return
fire and with at least 36 energy left after the shot. It can use the
gun's lead when the wider arc still covers the target. The quiet period,
shelter fire suppression, sprint policy, and laser charging rule remain
in the existing controller.

In four physical paired runs after the same arming and first kill, actual
arc hits during recovery increased from 6 to 8, 3 to 7, 6 to 11, and 7 to 13.
The extra shots used 9–18 additional energy. The second opponent ended with
less HP in every paired run. One new run cleared both waves with 167 HP.
Other runs still died, including one that died about five seconds sooner.
This diagnostic measures the available defensive fire under live pressure.

All 147 local tests passed. The official checker answered 300/300 on time,
with a 6.5 ms maximum. Crowded and growing-body maxima were 18.45 and
22.29 ms.
