# Revision 51: reserve detours for valuable verified rewards

Revision 41 routed around obstacles to collect known kill rewards, then
scored 4.19 on the 4.31 controller. This version starts with the combined
first-fight equipment and upgrade candidate. It gives the optional route
budget only to compact, observed bundles of at least four cubes. Normal
direct pickup still follows the existing rules.

In a public-map three-wave diagnostic, the stronger early candidate
defeats both initial opponents in all four cases. The only pilot that clears
the third wave receives the second opponent's five cubes. In the other
three cases, the third hunter takes them, grows from 6 to 11 cubes, and
gains a damage upgrade. These outcomes motivate testing the ownership of
a major reward. They do not isolate the whole placement effect.

The route still checks clearance for the enlarged body, race timing,
incoming damage, and the storm before committing to the pickup.
